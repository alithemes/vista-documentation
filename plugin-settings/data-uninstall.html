Data & Uninstall

By default, uninstalling Vista Portfolio keeps projects and plugin data.

Enable Delete data on uninstall in Settings if you want a full wipe when the plugin is removed.

Leave this off on production sites unless you intend to permanently delete projects, taxonomies, layouts, and options.
